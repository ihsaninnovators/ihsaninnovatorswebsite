Our Journey

A simple timeline of how our team was created

2024
- 100% Done
  • November: 3-person core team established, circulation of initial ideas & brainstorming within the core team happened during this month.
  • December: The first Programming Lead committed to the team, expanding the core group to four members. Continued development of ideas, concepts and recruitment strategies.
2025
- 0% Done
  • January: 2nd Programming lead committed to joining. 5-person core team established. Discussions with EIC were initiated regarding funding and facility utilization. The development of initial interest forms and logistical documentation began.
  • March: The non-profit incorporation process was launched. Internal team development continues. Finalization of promotional flyers and interest forms progressed.
  • April: Continuation of the non-profit application process. Fieldwork commenced to gauge interest, and final materials for recruitment were completed.
  • May: Official non-profit status successfully acquired. The initial interest form is distributed via newsletter to over 1,700 subscribers, commencing the recruitment phase.
  • June: Successful outreach yielded approximately 20 interested individuals. The interview process was later held and completed, and the official team roster was finalized. The official team website was launched, and the pursuit of external funding continued.
